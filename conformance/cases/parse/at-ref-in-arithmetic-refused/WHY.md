# Why this case exists

§4.2 rule 5, the same sentence: `@` is "**Not in arithmetic**", because "in a
header-cell formula a bare `ident` already means this row's value of that
column. `@c` there would be a second spelling of `c`, and §4.1.6's objection to
a second spelling applies unchanged."

§9.20 lists "an `@` outside a predicate" among the malformed column formulas.
